### Key Insights

* AI adoption has grown across different industries in recent years.
* Higher AI adoption does not always mean more layoffs.
* Recession periods often have more layoffs and hiring freezes.
* Big Tech and large companies make up a major part of the layoffs.
* Remote job opportunities are different across countries.
* Employee sentiment tends to decrease when layoffs increase.
* Revenue and stock performance vary across industries.

### Business Recommendations

* Focus on employee upskilling while adopting AI.
* Consider market conditions before making workforce decisions.
* Track employee sentiment during major organizational changes.
* Explore remote hiring in regions with good talent availability.
* Focus hiring on roles with growing demand, especially AI and ML.

### Conclusion

This project analyzes global layoffs, hiring trends, and AI adoption using EDA. I worked on data cleaning, feature engineering, visualization, encoding, and scaling to understand the patterns in the data.

The analysis helped identify how factors such as market conditions, company size, and AI adoption are connected with hiring and layoff trends.
